### Water walking

_Pure Faith allows the devotee to walk on water._

| Water walking |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1-4 |
| **Effect:** Target can walk on water. 1 target per faith point spent. |

Target can walk on water. 1 target per faith point spent. Choose up to 4 devotees. For the duration of the prayer they can walk on water. Treat the water as difficult ground (i.e. half speed). The cost to invoke this prayer is equal to the number of devotees selected.
