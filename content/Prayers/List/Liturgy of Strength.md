### Liturgy of Strength

_Your liturgy bolsters strength in your devotees._

| Liturgy of Strength |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **Invoking TN:** 13 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1-4 |
| **Effect:** Double Strength on damage. Cost 1 faith point per devotee buffed. |

Double Strength on damage. Cost 1 faith point per devotee buffed. The target(s) of this prayer double strength on the damage code of any attacks for the duration of the prayer. The cost to invoke this prayer is equal to the number of targets selected.
