### Icon of Protection

_You hold an icon of a Saint to protect you and your devotees from suffering._

| Icon of Protection |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 2 |
| **Invoking TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** all devotees |
| **Effect:** Gain a pool of WP d6. Invoker and allies may spend these dice to reduce damage. |

Gain a pool of WP d6. Invoker and allies may spend these dice to reduce damage. You gain a pool of WP d6 dice. For the duration of the prayer, you and your devotees may spend any number of dice after suffering damage to roll the selected dice and reduce the amount of damage. You may decide how many dice to spend only once per attack. Any unspent dice at the end of the duration are lost.
