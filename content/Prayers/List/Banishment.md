### Banishment

_Your mere presence banishes the heretics._

| Banishment |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 3 |
| **Invoking TN:** 19 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** 1 round |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** Area |
| **Effect:** Make pray vs spell TN test to make heretics in the area flee for 1 round |

Make pray vs spell TN test to make heretics in the area flee for 1 round You make a pray test. Any heretic in the area within range of the prayer with a spell TN equal to or lower than the prayer test result must flee on their next round.
