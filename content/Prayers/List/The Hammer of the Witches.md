### The Hammer of the Witches

_Malleus maleficarum: the hammer of the witches slays all heretics who practice the foul dark arts._

| The Hammer of the Witches |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 4 |
| **Invoking TN:** 21 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Melee weapon becomes magical, may make 1 extra melee attack per turn. |

Melee weapon becomes magical, may make 1 extra melee attack per turn. You bless 1 melee weapon which becomes magical. The wielder of the weapon may make 1 extra melee attack per turn. The weapon does not need to be an actual hammer, any melee weapon will do.
