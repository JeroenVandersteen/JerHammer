### Protection from Witchcraft

_Your devotee is protected from foul witchcraft._

| Protection from Witchcraft |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 2 |
| **Invoking TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Target gains additional spell TN equal to the invoker's WP. |

Target gains additional spell TN equal to the invoker's WP. The target devotee (may be the invoker) gains an additional spell TN equal to the invoker's WP.
