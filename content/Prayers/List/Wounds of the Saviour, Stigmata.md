### Wounds of the Saviour, Stigmata

_The heretic suffers the wounds of our Saviour, bleeding from arms and feet._

| Wounds of the Saviour, Stigmata |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 4 |
| **TN:** 21 |
| **Type:** Offense |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Choose a target in short range. Make pray vs spell TN test. Wounds appear, the target gains 4 bleeding conditions |

Choose a target in short range. Make pray vs spell TN test. Wounds appear, the target gains 4 bleeding conditions Choose a target heretic in a short range distance. Make a pray test versus the target's spell TN. If successful, wounds appear on the heretic's wrists and feet, causing 4 bleeding conditions on the target.
