### Sword of Justice

_Your devotees' weapons are empowered by justice._

| Sword of Justice |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1-4 |
| **Effect:** May reroll any 6 and add, for to hit and damage rolls; cost 1 faith point per target |

May reroll any 6 and add, for to hit and damage rolls; cost 1 faith point per target The target(s) of this prayer may reroll any 6 rolled on to hit and damage rolls, and keep adding to the result, for the duration of the prayer. The cost to invoke this prayer is equal to the number of targets selected.
