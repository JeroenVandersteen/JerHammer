### Crown of Thorns

_You place a crown of thorns on the heretic's head to make them feel the suffering of our Saviour._

| Crown of Thorns |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 3 |
| **Invoking TN:** 17 |
| **Type:** Offense |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Touch |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Choose a target in melee range. Make pray vs melee TN test. If successful, target gains 7d6 L damage and 1 bleeding condition. |

Choose a target in melee range. Make pray vs melee TN test. If successful, target gains 7d6 L damage and 1 bleeding condition. Choose a target heretic in melee range. Make a pray test versus the target's melee TN. If successful, the target suffers 7d6 L damage (armor applies) and gains 1 bleeding condition.
