### Consacrate Oath

_You consacrate an oath, which cannot be broken without long-lasting consequences._

| Consacrate Oath |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 2 |
| **Invoking TN:** 17 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Touch |
| **Target:** all touched |
| **Effect:** Forces all targets touched during the prayer to keep their oath, or permanently lose 1 Toughness point |

Forces all targets touched during the prayer to keep their oath, or permanently lose 1 Toughness point The subjects of this prayer willingly make an oath while touching the invoker. This forces all targets to keep their oath, or permanently lose 1 Toughness point when they do break it. Any number of subjects can be included, as long as they touch the invoker and speak their oath out loud.
