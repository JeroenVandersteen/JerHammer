### Snake branch

_You throw branches on the ground. When touching the ground, they turn into slithering snakes, attacking the heretics._

| Snake branch |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **Invoking TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Summon |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** - |
| **Effect:** Throw 1 to 4 branches on ground, each branch becomes WP snake minions. Cost 1 faith point per branch. |

Throw 1 to 4 branches on ground, each branch becomes WP snake minions. Cost 1 faith point per branch. You may throw up to 4 branches on the ground. Each branch turns into WP snake minions, that follow your commands to attack heretics. The snakes may attack immediately. At the end of the duration of the prayer, any unkilled snakes turn back into regular branches.
