### Our lady of Innocence

_You and your devotees are inspired to excel by our Lady of Innocence._

| Our lady of Innocence |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **Invoking TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Buff |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** all devotees |
| **Effect:** Gain a pool of dice equal to Faith points spent. Each die can be spent by any devotee to reroll 1d6 on any roll. |

Gain a pool of dice equal to Faith points spent. Each die can be spent by any devotee to reroll 1d6 on any roll. You create a dice pool with a number of dice equal to the number of faith points spent (between 1 to 4). For the duration of the prayer, you or any or your devotees can spend one of the dice to reroll a single d6 on any roll. Multiple pool dice can be used on the same roll. Spent dice are lost. Any unspent dice at the end of the prayer duration are lost.
