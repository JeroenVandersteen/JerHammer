### Divine intervention

_God intervenes to fling away the heretics._

| Divine intervention |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 1 |
| **Invoking TN:** 15 |
| **Type:** Offense |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1-4 |
| **Effect:** Make opposed pray vs Athletics test to fling heretic(s) away by WP meters and gain prone condition. Costs 1 faith point per target. |

Make opposed pray vs Athletics test to fling heretic(s) away by WP meters and gain prone condition. Costs 1 faith point per target. Select up to 4 heretics in a short range distance as targets. You make a pray test, opposed by Athletics by each of the heretics. Each heretic that fails, is flung away WP meters in a direction of your chosing, and gains the prone condition. Additional damage or effects may occur, depending on the location where the heretic lands. The cost to invoke this prayer is equal to the number of targets selected.
