### Rainy day

***Level:** 1

**Duration:** 1d6 days

_It always rains on the victim, 2m radius around them_

When outdoors, a small cloud keeps hovering right above the target of the curse, and keeps raining in about a 2m radius around them. Indoors, there is constantly some water (or worse) dripping from the ceiling on the target.
