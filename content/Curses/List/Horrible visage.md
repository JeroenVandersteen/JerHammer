### Horrible visage

***Level:** 3

**Duration:** 3d6 days

_Slowly get uglier: warts, bumps, bad pose, hair loss, wrinkles,..._

The target of the curse gets uglier over time, and grows warts, bumps, develops a bad pose and hair loss, gains wrinkles,... This causes fear, superstition and loathing in other people. The exact effect is left up to the GM.
