### Sticky hands

***Level:** 4

**Duration:** 4d6 days

_Hands become very sticky, hard to release anything touched_

The target's hands become extremely sticky, even through gloves or metal gauntlets. It takes a sleight of hands test to remove something from their hands. On a failed test, the target's hand(s) suffer 1d6L damage, or any gloves worn are torn apart. A successful strength test also removes any item from their hand, but with again the same 1d6L damage or torn gloves.
