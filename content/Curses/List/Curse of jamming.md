### Curse of jamming

***Level:** 1

**Duration:** 1d6 days

_Blackpowder weapons have 50% chance to misfire_

Whenever the target of the curse fires a blackpowder weapon, there is a 50% chance the weapon misfires (e.g. on a 1d6 roll of 1-3). When the weapon misfires, use the war machine misfire table to see its effect.
