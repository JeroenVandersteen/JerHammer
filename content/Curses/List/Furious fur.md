### Furious fur

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Grows unnatural amount of hair and fur everywhere_

The target of the curse grows an unnatural amount of hair and fur all over their body. This is difficult to hide, and when spotted by others may lead to superstitution or suspicions of being a mutant.
