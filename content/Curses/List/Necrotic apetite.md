### Necrotic apetite

***Level:** 3

**Duration:** 3d6 days

_Can eat only rotting meat, no other food_

The target of the curse only gains nutrients from rotten and necrotic meat, not from normal food anymore. If the target doesn't consume rotten meat, they suffer from hunger and gain a fatigue condition each day, which can only be removed by consuming rotten meat again.
