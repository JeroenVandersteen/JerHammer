### Insomnia

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Insomnia, suffer 1 fatigue condition every morning, max 3._

The target suffers from insomnia, unable to fall asleep. They wake up every morning with an additional fatigue condition, with a maximum of 3.
