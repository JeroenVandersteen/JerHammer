### Slippery feet

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Make acrobatics test every turn or slip and fall_

The target of the curse must make an acrobatics tests every turn while moving faster than walking speed, or while in combat. On a failed roll, the target slips and falls prone.
