### Rust

***Level:** 4

**Duration:** 4d6 days

_Metal rusts in your hands_

Any metal touched by the target of the curse rusts very rapidly (over the course of a few minutes to hours). This effect even goes through clothing and armor worn by the target; though slightly slowed down.
