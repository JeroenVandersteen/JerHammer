### Empathic pain

***Level:** 3

**Duration:** 3d6 days

_Take 1 HP dam whenever anyone in short range takes dam_

The target of the curse suffers 1 point of damage whenever any other creature of a similar species in short range takes damage, friend or foe. The type of damage (lethal or impact) is the same type as the original damage. Halflings, gnomes, elfs, dwarfs and humans are all considered similar species for the purpose of this curse. Greenskins and humans are not considered similar species.
