### Foul smell

***Level:** 1

**Duration:** 1d6 days

_Stinks like a sewer full of decaying flesh_

The target of the curse gains a foul smell, like a sewer full of decaying flesh. To most species this smells horrible; though some creatures may be attracted by it.
