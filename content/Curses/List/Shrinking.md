### Shrinking

***Level:** 3

**Duration:** 3d6 days

_Reduce size by 2 steps (lower HP, etc)_

The target of the curse shrinks in size by 2 steps. E.g. a medium sized creature becomes tiny. The HP, damage penalties etc are re-calculated accordingly.
