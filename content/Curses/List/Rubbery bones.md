### Rubbery bones

***Level:** 3

**Duration:** 3d6 days

_Bones get very flexible, penalty to S, bonus to Ag_

The target's bones get very flexible and malleable. They suffer disadvantage to any to hit rolls, strength-based tests, and damage rolls; but gain advantage to Agility-based tests.
