### Second chance for failure

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Forced to reroll a successful to hit roll once every roll_

The target of the curse must reroll a successful to hit roll (in ranged combat, melee combat, or as part of casting a spell) once per turn. If the reroll is still successful, the attack hits normally.
