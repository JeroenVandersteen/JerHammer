### Insatiable apetite

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Insatiable apetite, irrationally compelled to eat flesh and drink blood_

The target of the curse suffers from an insatiable apetite for flesh and blood. Normal food still provides any nutrients, but the target has no apetite for it.
