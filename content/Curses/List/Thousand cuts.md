### Thousand cuts

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Target gets 1d6 L wounds every day when waking up_

The target of the curse wakes up every morning with a thousand small cuts, and suffers 1d6 lethal damage every time (not modified by armor points).
