### Sunlight sensitivity

***Level:** 2

**Duration:** 2d6 days

_Sensitive to Sunlight: disadv in Sunlight, 1d6L wounds per hour_

The target of the curse becomes sensitive to sunlight. While in direct sunlight, the target suffers disadvantage to all tests, and suffers 1d6 L wounds per hour (armor points do not apply). These penalties do not apply indoors, in thick shadow, during heavy rain, or at dusk/dawn of overcast days.
