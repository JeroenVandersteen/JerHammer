### Slowness of tongue

***Level:** 1

**Duration:** 1d6 days

_Must speak very slowly_

The target of the curse starts speaking very slowly. At the GM's discretion this may lead to hilarious, annoying or downright embarrassing situtations and appropriate penalties.
