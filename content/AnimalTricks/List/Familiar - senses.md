### Familiar - senses

_Caster may use the familiar's senses instead of their own_ 

The caster may use the familiar's senses (sight, smell, hearing, touch, taste) instead of their own. While using the familiar's senses, the caster's body remains in the pose it was before, but is completely unaware of its surroundings. The caster snaps out of this state as soon as they take any damage.
