### Rifle-bore

**Target Item:** Firearm

**Level:** 4

**Cost:** 48 cp

_Reduce range pen. by 1, +1 to hit_

This blackpowder weapon uses a rifle-bore barrel instead of smoothbore barrel. This reduces the range penalty by 1 range increment and provides a +1 bonus to hit.
