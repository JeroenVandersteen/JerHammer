### Explosive bolt-arrow

**Target Item:** Bolt or arrow

**Level:** 2

**Cost:** 12 cp

_Gain +2d6 L dam, one-time use_

The arrow or bolt is fitted with an explosive, detonating on impact. If successfully hit, the explosion causes an additional 2d6 L damage.
