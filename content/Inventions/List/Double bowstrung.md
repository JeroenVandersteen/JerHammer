### Double bowstrung

**Target Item:** Crossbow

**Level:** 3

**Cost:** 24 cp

_May fire twice in 1 round_

Two bowstrungs are mounted on this crossbow. You may fire twice before needing to reload. You may fire twice in 1 round, both shots are at disadvantage.
