### Barbed wire wrapped

**Target Item:** Melee Weapon

**Level:** 4

**Cost:** 48d

_Cause bleed on hit, gets stuck on roll 3-6_

The melee weapon is wrapped with barbed wire. This causes an extra bleed condition on a hit. On a roll of 3 to 6, the weapon gets stuck in the opponent. It take a successful strength test (TN 15) to pry the weapon loose. This test may be attempted once per round (including on the round it got stuck). The weapon may be dropped at any time as a free action.
