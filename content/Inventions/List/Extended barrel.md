### Extended barrel

**Target Item:** Firearm

**Level:** 2

**Cost:** 12 cp

_Reduce range penalty by 1 increm._

The extended barrel allows for an increase accuracy at long range. Reduce the range penalties by 1 range increment. The weapon loses the pistol quality.
