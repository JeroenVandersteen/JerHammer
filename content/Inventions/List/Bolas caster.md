### Bolas caster

**Target Item:** Bow, crossbow, firearm

**Level:** 2

**Cost:** 12 cp

_Shoots bolas_

The weapon can also launch a bolas. The bolas has short range, S+1d6 impact damage, and the trip quality. When firing both the original weapon's ammo and the bolas in one turn, both shots are at disadvantage.
