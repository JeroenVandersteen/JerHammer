### Pistolier

_Only offhand pistol at disadvantage_

Only the offhand has disadvantage when using ranged weapons with the pistol quality.

**Without this training:**
Without this training, both main hand and offhand are at disadvantage when using two ranged weapons (both with the pistol quality).
