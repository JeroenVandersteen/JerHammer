### Arcane Magick III
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Magick II

_Cast level 3 spells from 1 lore, gain 1 spell_

You may cast level 3 spells from any one Lore for which you already have the level 2 training. When you take this training, you immediately learn 1 level 3 spell. 

You may gather up to level 3 spell ingredients when choosing that endeavor.
