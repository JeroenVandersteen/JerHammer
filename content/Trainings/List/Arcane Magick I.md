### Arcane Magick I
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Cast level 1 spells from 1 lore, gain 3 spells_

You may cast level 1 spells from any one Lore of Magick. When you take this training, you immediately learn 3 level 1 spells. You may learn additional level 1 spells for 50 XP each (or 25 XP if you find that spell in a grimoire). You may take this talent multiple times; each time selecting a different lore of Magick. You may not learn the pray Trainings when you have any Arcane Magic Training. You can unlearn this Training for free but will immediately lose all of your spells if you do so.

Additionally, you may gather spell ingredients as an endeavor. You may gather a level 1 spell ingredient per endeavor.
