### Holy hatred (group)
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Prayers deal +1d6 dam vs chosen group_

Your prayers deal an extra +1d6 damage against enemies of the chosen group. Examples of groups are greenskins, undead, elfs, dwarfs, halflings, beastmen, skaven, shapeshifters. You cannot pick humans.
