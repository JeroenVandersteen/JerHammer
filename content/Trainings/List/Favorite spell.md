### Favorite spell
**Max:** Int
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Choose one spell: TN-1_

Choose one spell. You can cast that spell as if it has a TN 1 lower for each time you have taken this training. You may choose the same spell multiple times, each time reducing the TN by one, but never less than 15. You cannot chose a level 1 spell for this.
