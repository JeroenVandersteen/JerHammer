### Poisoner
**Max:** Int, 4
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Can create poisons_

You can use an endeavour to create poisons up to a combined level of poisons equal to the number of times the training was taken. You have to pay the listed cost for ingredients, use of tools and workshop. You can call yourself apprentice (I), journeyman (II), master (III) or grandmaster (IV), depending on the number of times this training was taken.
