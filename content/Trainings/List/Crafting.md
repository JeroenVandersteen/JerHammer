### Crafting
**Max:** Int, 4
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Craft items (50% price)_

You can use an endeavour to craft up to a combined level of items equal to the number of times the training was taken, at 50% of the normal item prices. You can call yourself apprentice (I), journeyman (II), master (III) or grandmaster (IV), depending on the number of times this training was taken.
