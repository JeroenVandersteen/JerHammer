### Arcane Magick II
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Magick I

_Cast level 2 spells from 1 lore, gain 1 spell_

You may cast level 2 spells from any one Lore for which you already have the level 1 training. When you take this training, you immediately learn 1 level 2 spell.

You may gather up to level 2 spell ingredients when choosing that endeavor.
