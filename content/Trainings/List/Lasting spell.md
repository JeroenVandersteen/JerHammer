### Lasting spell
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Any spell: increase duration, TN+1_

You may choose to cast any spell as if it has a TN 1 higher. When you do so, you may increase the duration of the spell by an additional nominal duration. Spells with a duration of instant or permanent are not affected. You must clearly declare that you are using this training before rolling the spellcast test.
