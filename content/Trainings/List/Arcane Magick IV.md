### Arcane Magick IV
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Magick III

_Cast level 4 spells from 1 lore, gain 3 spells_

You may cast level 4 spells from any one Lore for which you already have the level 3 training. When you take this training, you immediately learn 3 level 4 spells. You may learn additional level 4 spells for 75 XP each (or 50 XP if you find that spell in a grimoire).

Additionally, you may gather spell ingredients as an endeavor. You may gather up to a combined 4 levels of spell ingredients per endeavor.
