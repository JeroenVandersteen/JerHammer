### Arcane Magick IV
**Prerequisites:** Arcane Magick III

_Cast level 4 spells from 1 lore, gain 1 spell_

You may cast level 4 spells from any one Lore for which you already have the level 3 training. When you take this training, you immediately learn 1 level 4 spell.

You may gather up to level 4 spell ingredients when choosing that endeavor.
