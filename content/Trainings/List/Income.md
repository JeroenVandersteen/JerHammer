### Income
**Max:** Level+2

_Increase income per game session_

You increase the amount of income you receive at the start of each session, and when using the income endeavor. Additionally, your lifestyle is increased for all aspects where individual money is not tracked (such as drinks, meals and lodging in an inn). Finally, also your social class is increased. Characters with a lower income level generally look up to you; in particular if the difference in income level is greater. Your income level may not be higher than your highest profession level + 2.
