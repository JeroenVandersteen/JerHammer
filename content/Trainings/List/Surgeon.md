### Surgeon

_Medicine test and 1 hour to heal double HP/day_

Spend a full hour doing surgery and make a medicine test. On a successful test, the target heals double the normal amount of HP/day. If failed, the target loses 1d6 HP. The target cannot be yourself. This can only be attempted once per character per injury. Using (and spending) bandages gives an advantage to the medicine test.
