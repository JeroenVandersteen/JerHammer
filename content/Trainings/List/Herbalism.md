### Herbalism
**Max:** Int, 4
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Can gather herbs_

You can use an endeavour to gather herbs up to a combined level of herbs equal to the number of times the training was taken. You have to pay the listed cost for other ingredients, use of tools and workshop. You can call yourself apprentice (I), journeyman (II), master (III) or grandmaster (IV), depending on the number of times this training was taken.
