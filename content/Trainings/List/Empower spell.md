### Empower spell
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Any spell: reroll damage, TN+1_

You may choose to cast any spell as if it has a TN 1 higher. When you do so, you can reroll the damage dice, and choose the highest of the two rolls. Spells that do not deal damage are not affected. You must clearly declare that you are using this training before rolling the spellcast test.
