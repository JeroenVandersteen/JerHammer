### Battle wizard
**Max:** WP
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Armor pen. 1 less; no miscast disadv in melee_

For every time you take this training, the armor penalties to the spellcasting test are reduced by 1. Additionally, you do not gain disadvantage on your miscast rolls when casting while in a melee combat.

**Without this training:**
Without this training, spellcasters receive an armor penalty to their casting roll. Without this training, when casting while engaged in a melee combat, you roll your miscast with disadvantage.
