### Battle wizard
**Max:** WP
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Armor pen. 1 less; no miscast disadv in melee_

For every time you take this training, the armor penalties to the spellcasting test are reduced by 1. Additionally, you do not gain disadvantage on your micast rolls when casting while in a melee combat.

**Without this training:**
Without this training, spellcasters receive a penalty to their casting roll equal to the number of armor points worn, or twice the impact reduction points, whichever is higher. Without this training, when casting while engaged in a melee combat, you roll your miscast with disadvantage.
