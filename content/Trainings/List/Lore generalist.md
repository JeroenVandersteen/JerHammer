### Lore generalist

_Have a 1-in-3 chance to have any lore_

The character has an excellent all-round knowledge. Whenever a lore test is required or is a lore or provides any benefits, there is a 1 in 3 chance (a roll of 5+ on 1d6) that the character by chance knows the relevant information as if they have that lore.
