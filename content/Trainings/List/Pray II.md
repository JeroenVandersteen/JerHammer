### Pray II
**Prerequisites:** Pray I

_Can invoke lvl 2 prayers, 2xWP faith point max_

You may invoke level 2 prayers. When you take this talent, you immediately learn 1 prayers of level 2. You may learn additional level 2 prayers at a cost of 75 XP (or 50 XP if you find that prayer in a holy scripture).

You gain a faith point maximum equal to 2x your WP. You can use faith points to invoke prayers, at the cost of 1 faith point per level of the prayer. You regain 1 faith point per hour spent praying to your God, up to your faith point maximum. If you spend 1 hour praying in a chapel, church or otherwise sacred location, you regain all your faith points up to the maximum.
