### Roundhouse attack

_Divide dam on all that are hit_

You make a single melee attack roll and divide the damage among all the opponents that are in range and that are hit by your attack roll.

This training cannot be combined with assassinate, brutal strike, cleave, deathstrike, drilled, entangling strike, execute, furious assault, grab weapon, jump strike, knockout strike, pull closer, powerful strike, push away, relentless charge, riposte, shield push, smash hard, two-weapon fighting.
