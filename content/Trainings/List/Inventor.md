### Inventor
**Max:** Int, 4
**Prerequisites:** Profession

_Make item modifications_

You can use an endeavour to make item modifications up to a combined level of modifications equal to the number of times the training was taken. You have to pay the listed cost for materials, use of tools and workshop. You can call yourself apprentice (I), journeyman (II), master (III) or grandmaster (IV), depending on the number of times this training was taken.
