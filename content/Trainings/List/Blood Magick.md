### Blood Magick
**Max:** WP

_Take HP-1 for TN-1_

You may choose to take 1 lethal wound per time you have taken this training. If you do so, you may cast any one spell that turn at a TN that is 1 lower per wound. You must clearly declare that you are using this training before rolling the spellcast test.
