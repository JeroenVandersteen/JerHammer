### Dervish attack
**Prerequisites:** Roundhouse attack

_Deal full dam on all that are hit_

You make a single melee attack roll and deal the full amount of damage on all opponents that are in range and that are hit by the roll. Afterwards all opponents in melee reach get 1 free melee attack on you as reaction.

This training cannot be combined with assassinate, brutal strike, cleave, deathstrike, drilled, entangling strike, execute, furious assault, grab weapon, jump strike, knockout strike, pull closer, powerful strike, push away, relentless charge, riposte, shield push, smash hard, two-weapon fighting.
