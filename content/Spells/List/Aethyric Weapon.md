### Aethyric Weapon

_From the mists of arcane mastery, a blade of pure energy coalesces, its edge honed to cut through the fabric of reality itself._

| Aethyric Weapon |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Arcane Magic |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Self |
| **Effect:** Create a melee weapon Int+3d6, magical, choose lethal or impact |

 The caster creates a magical melee weapon that deals damage equal to their Intelligence (Int) plus 3d6. The caster can choose between dealing lethal or impact damage.
