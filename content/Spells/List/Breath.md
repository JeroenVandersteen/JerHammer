### Breath

_From the depths of the mage's soul, a torrent of elemental power erupts, consuming all in its path with the searing embrace of pure magic._

| Breath |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Arcane Magic |
| **CN:** 6 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** Area |
| **Damage:** 3d6 L |
| **Armor:** AP counts |
| **Resist:** Dodge TN |
| **Effect:** Combat spell.  |

This spell deals 3d6 L (AP counts) to all creatures in an area at short range instantly. The spellcast check has to also be equal to or higher than the target's Dodge TN. The caster exhales a destructive breath, dealing damage to targets caught in its path.
