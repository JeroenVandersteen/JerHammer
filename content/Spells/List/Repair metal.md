### Repair metal

_In the hands of the smith, metal bends to will, whether to heal the broken or sunder the unyielding, as the dance of creation unfolds._

| Repair metal |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 3 |
| **Lore:** Terrestrial Elements |
| **Casting TN:** 19 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Touch |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Repair or break a metal object (armor, broken weapon) or craft metal item at 50% of cost |

 Allows the caster to repair or break a metal object, such as armor or a broken weapon, or craft a new metal item at 50% of its normal cost.
