### Fire cloak

_Enveloping oneself in flames as a protective barrier, warding off assailants with searing heat._

| Fire cloak |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 3 |
| **Lore:** Fire Element |
| **Casting TN:** 19 |
| **Type:** Enchantment |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Self |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Become immune to non-magical fire and ablaze conditions, opponents must make dodge test or suffer ablaze when hitting caster. |

 Upon casting, the caster becomes immune to non-magical fire and ablaze conditions. Opponents engaging the caster in melee combat must make a successful dodge test or suffer the ablaze condition upon hitting the caster, taking additional damage over time.
