### Arcane Urgency

_In the blink of an eye, the world blurs and shifts as the target moves with preternatural speed, a fleeting glimpse of the mage's unyielding will._

| Arcane Urgency |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Terrestrial Elements |

| **Type:** Enchantment |

| **Range:** Short |
