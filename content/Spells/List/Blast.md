### Blast

_With a whispered incantation, the air crackles with arcane energy, unleashing a tempest of power to rend flesh and sunder bone._

| Blast |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Arcane Magic |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** Area |
| **Damage:** 2d6 L/i |
| **Armor:** AP/IR counts |
| **Resist:** Spell TN |
| **Effect:** Combat spell. Caster chooses lethal or impact |

This spell deals 2d6 L/i (AP/IR counts) to all creatures in an area at short range instantly. The spellcast check has to also be equal to or higher than the target's Spell TN. The caster unleashes a blast of magical energy, dealing damage to all creatures in an area with damage type of their choosing between lethal or impact.
