### Wandering shadow

_Your shadow moves as if it has a mind of its own._

| Wandering shadow |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Darkness |
| **CN:** 0 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** minutes |
| **Range:** Self |
| **Effect:** Shadow moves independently from body and may interact with objects/creatures within short range and strength of 1. |

 Your shadow moves around independently from your body, and may interact with objects and creatures with an equivalent strength of 1. You may watch through your shadow's eyes, but if you do so, you gain half speed and disadvantage to all your own rolls.
