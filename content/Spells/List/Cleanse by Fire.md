### Cleanse by Fire

_Purifying through flames, consuming impurities and leaving behind only ash in its wake._

| Cleanse by Fire |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Lore Of Fire |
| **CN:** 6 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** Area |
| **Effect:** Cleanse area of corruption and prevent bodies to rise as undead |

 This spell purges corruption from the area, preventing bodies from rising as undead. Any undead attempting to rise within the cleansed area are immediately destroyed.
