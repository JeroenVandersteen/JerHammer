### Control blood iron

_In the crucible of flesh, iron stirs, seeking escape from mortal coil, as the body becomes the battleground of alchemical upheaval._

| Control blood iron |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Heart |
| **CN:** 9 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Damage:** 4d6 L |
| **Armor:** AP ignored |
| **Resist:** Spell TN |
| **Effect:** Combat spell. Iron in target's blood starts fighting its way out |

This spell deals 4d6 L (AP ignored) to 1 target at short range instantly. The spellcast check has to also be equal to or higher than the target's Spell TN. Initiates a process where iron in the target's blood begins to fight its way out, causing internal harm.
