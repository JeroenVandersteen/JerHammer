### Necrotic Husk

| Necrotic Husk |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 4 |
| **Lore:** Death |
| **Casting TN:** 21 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** Area |
| **Damage:** 4d6 L |
| **Armor:** AP ignored |
| **Resist:** Dodge TN |
| **Effect:** Combat spell. When you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can use a reaction (consume next round's action) to drop to 1 hit point instead and cause your body to expel necrotic energy. Each creature of your choice in short range takes 4d6 L necrotic damage |

This spell deals 4d6 L (AP ignored) to all creatures in an area at short range instantly. The spellcast check has to also be equal to or higher than the target's Dodge TN.
