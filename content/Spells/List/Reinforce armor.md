### Reinforce armor

_In the forge's embrace, the armor is tempered anew, each strike imbuing it with the resilience to withstand even the fiercest of blows._

| Reinforce armor |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Lore Of Metal |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Enchantment |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Touch |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Effect:** Armor gains +10 AP and +3 IR |

 Increases the armor points (AP) of one set of armor by 10 and grants an additional 3 points of impact reduction (IR).
