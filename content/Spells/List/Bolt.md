### Bolt

_Like lightning from the heavens, a bolt of raw magic streaks forth, seeking its mark with unerring precision, bringing destruction in its wake_

| Bolt |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Arcane Magic |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** instant |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Damage:** 4d6 L/i |
| **Armor:** AP/IR counts |
| **Resist:** Spell TN |
| **Effect:** Combat spell. Caster chooses lethal or impact |

This spell deals 4d6 L/i (AP/IR counts) to 1 target at short range instantly. The spellcast check has to also be equal to or higher than the target's Spell TN. Similar to Blast, but the damage is focused into a bolt of energy that strikes a single target.
