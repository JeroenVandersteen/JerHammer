### Summon fire elemental

_Calling forth a creature of pure flame and rage from the depths of the inferno to do the caster's bidding._

| Summon fire elemental |
| :--- |
| **Level:** 4 |
| **Lore:** Heavenly Elements |
| **Casting TN:** 21 |
| **Type:** Summon |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Effect:** Summon fire elemental |

 By invoking this spell, the caster summons a powerful elemental creature composed of fire to their aid. The fire elemental obeys the caster's commands and remains under their control for the duration of the spell or until it is defeated in combat.
