### Aethyric Armor

_As shadows gather, the mage's will manifests, weaving ethereal protection, a shimmering shield against impending harm._

| Aethyric Armor |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Arcane Magic |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Enchantment |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Self |
| **Effect:** Gain (3xWP) APs and 3 points of IR |

 The caster gains armor points equal to three times their Willpower (WP) and three points of impact reduction.
