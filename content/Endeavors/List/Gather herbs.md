### Gather herbs

**Prerequisite:** [Herbalism](<Trainings/List/Herbalism>) talent

_Gather herbs, pay for ingredients, tools_

As per the talent, you may gather a combined level of herbs up to the number of times you have taken the gather herbs talent. You have to pay the listed price for the use of tools and other processing ingredients.
