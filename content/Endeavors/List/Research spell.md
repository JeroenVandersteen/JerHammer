### Research spell

**Prerequisite:** [Arcane Magick](<Trainings/List/Arcane Magick>) talent

_Learn 1 spell, at a cost_

Spellcasters may spend an endeavor to research new spells within their trained lore, paying a cost to cover for experiment tools, materials, library use, bribing to obtain secret knowledges etc. The cost for learning new spells is given in the table below:
- New Spell: 3 st
-New Spell from Grimoire: 2 st
