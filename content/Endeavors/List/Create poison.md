### Create poison

**Prerequisite:** [Poisoner](<Trainings/List/Poisoner>) talent

_Create poisons, pay for ingredients, tools_

As per the talent, you may create a combined level of poisons up to the number of twice times you have taken the create poison talent. You have to pay the listed price for the ingredients, use of tools and workshop.
