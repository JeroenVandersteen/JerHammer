### Study subject

**Prerequisite:** 

_After 3 endeavors, gain 1 Lore_

You apply yourself to studying a subject deeply. After spending 3 endeavors studying a subject, you permanently gain one lore of your choice. Studying can be done through library research, field research, interviewing experts, experimentation, etc. The 3 endeavors do not need to be consecutive.
