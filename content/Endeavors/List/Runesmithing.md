### Runesmithing

**Prerequisite:** [Runesmith](<Trainings/List/Runesmith>) talent

_May add runes to items, pay for tools_

As per the talent, you may use an endeavor to add runes up to a combined level of runes equal to the number of times the talent was taken. You have to pay the listed price for the materials, use of tools and workshop.
