### Divination

**Prerequisite:** [Divination](<Talents/List/Divination>) talent

_Roll Int dice; can be used to swap_

As per the talent, you may roll a divination dice pool of Int dice. You may swap any die you roll later by one of the divination pool dice. Divination pool dice can be swapped only once and are spent afterwards. Any divination pool dice left over at the end of the session are lost.
