### Arcane Magick I
**Prerequisites:** Career

_Can cast level 1 arcane spells, all lores_

You may cast level 1 spells (CN 0) from any Lore of Magick. When you take this talent, you immediately learn WP level 1 spells. You may learn additional level 1 spells for 50 XP each (or 25 XP if you find that spell in a grimoire). You may not learn the Bless or Invoke Talents when you have any Arcane Magic Talent. You can unlearn this Talent for free but will immediately lose all of your spells if you do so.
