### Pray I
**Prerequisites:** Career

_Can invoke lvl 1 prayers, WP faith point max_

You may invoke level 1 prayers. When you take this talent, you immediately learn WP prayers of level 1. You may learn additional level 1 prayers at a cost of 50 XP (or 25 XP if you find that prayer in a holy scripture). You may not learn the Arcane Magick Talents when you have any pray Talent. You can unlearn this Talent for free but will immediately lose all of your prayers if you do so.

You gain a faith point maximum equal to your WP. You can use faith points to invoke prayers, at the cost of 1 faith point per level of the prayer. You regain 1 faith point per hour spent praying to your God, up to your faith point maximum. If you spend 1 hour praying in a chapel, church or otherwise sacred location, you regain all your faith points up to the maximum.
