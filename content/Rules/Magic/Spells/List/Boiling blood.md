### Boiling blood

_Igniting the blood within a target's veins, causing excruciating pain and potential internal damage._

| Boiling blood |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Lore Of Fire |
| **CN:** 6 |
| **Type:** Combat |
| **Duration:** rounds |
| **Range:** Short |
| **Target:** 1 |
| **Damage:** 2d6 L |
| **Armor:** AP ignored |
| **Resist:** Spell TN |
| **Effect:** Target's blood starts boiling for the whole duration |

The target's blood starts boiling, causing excruciating pain and dealing ongoing damage for the spell's duration. Each round, the target must make a successful Spell resistance test (TN 16) or suffer 2d6 L (Lethal) damage, ignoring Armor Points, until the spell ends or they receive appropriate medical attention.
