### Inextinguishable flame

_A flame that defies attempts to extinguish it, burning relentlessly without using fuel._

| Inextinguishable flame |
| :--- |
| **Lore:** Lore Of Fire |
| **CN:** 3 |
| **Type:** Utility |
| **Duration:** months |
| **Range:** Touch |
| **Effect:** Ignite a fire that can't be extinguished normally, no fuel needed, doesn't spread |

The caster ignites a fire that cannot be extinguished normally. This flame requires no fuel and does not spread beyond its initial area. It persists until dispelled by magical means or until the caster chooses to extinguish it.
